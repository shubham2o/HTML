What is a div tag?

• Whenever you want to make a rectangular shape / rectangular image on a website, then use div tag.

• It is called division tag, that lets you group similar sets of content together on a web page.

• A div tag has width of its own but it does not have its height.

What are HTML5 semantics?

• They are the tags that define the meaning of the content they contain.

• For example, tags like <header>, <article>, <footer>, <aside>, <section>, <video>, <nav>, <main> are examples of the HTML5 semantics. They clearly indicate the role of the content they contain.

• On the other hand, tags like <div>, <span> are typical examples of non-semantics HTML elements.

• They are indeed useful for both Google and other search engines, as they help them better understand the structure and content of web pages.

• By using semantic tags properly, you are likely to create a more organized and well-structured HTML document, which can indirectly contribute to better rendering on various devices, including mobile.
